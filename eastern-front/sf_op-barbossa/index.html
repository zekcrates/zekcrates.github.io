JUNE 22, 1941 — AUGUST 23, 1943

EASTERN FRONT

OPERATION BARBAROSSA  ·  THE BATTLE OF STALINGRAD

At 03:15 on June 22, 1941, 3.8 million Axis soldiers and 3,600 tanks crossed the Soviet frontier on a 2,900 km front — the largest invasion in history. By December they stood before Moscow. A year later, an entire German army vanished in the ruins of Stalingrad.
